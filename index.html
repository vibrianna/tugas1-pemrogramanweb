Discover the Apps

CapCut

CapCut is a video editing app suitable for beginners. The app offers various features such as video trimming, transitions, effects, text, music, and filters. It offers both free and paid features. Available on mobile and desktop.

Alight Motion

Alight Motion is an editing application that can be used to create animations, motion graphics, visual effects, and videos. It is easy to use and offers a variety of creative tools. It offers both free and paid features. Only available on mobile.

Adobe After Effects

Adobe After Effects is a professional software for creating animations, motion graphics, and visual effects. It offers advanced tools for detailed and high-quality editing. Only available on desktop and requires a paid subscription.
